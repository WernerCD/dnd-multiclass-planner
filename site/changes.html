Tool Changes

Multiclass Planner

Multiclass builds of up to three classes for 2014 and 2024 D&D 5e

Changes

What's new on the Multiclass Planner, newest first.

November 19, 2026

  • Dice rolling. Tap any dice in a feature (like Divine Smite's 2d8) to roll it, and tap a hit die under Hit dice to roll it with your Constitution modifier, as on a short rest. The roll tray keeps a log shared with the other D&D Toolbox tools.
  • Links to the other tools. Conditions in feature text open the Rules Reference, and feats you have taken open Feats & Origins.
  • Works offline. After one visit the planner opens with no signal, and you can add it to your home screen as an app.
  • Search from the hub. Every class and subclass shows up in the D&D Toolbox hub's "Search every tool" box and opens here with it picked.
  • Preview cards. Links to the planner show a picture card in chats and posts, and the site has proper app icons.
  • Tested before every update. Each update now runs a browser test suite before it goes live: every page at desktop and phone width, the menus and theme, characters and share links, and planner checks such as the level table, slots, charts, the three-class limit, Save as character and the D&D Beyond import.
  • Importing, the easy way. The D&D Beyond import dialog now also points to the D&D Toolbox Characters page, which imports D&D Beyond, Foundry VTT and Fight Club 5 characters once for every tool; the planner then offers to build from them.
  • Steadier menus. Clicking a top-bar menu right after hovering it no longer closes it again. The character sheet summary also gives your hit dice and class levels at your current level.
  • Tool menus in the top bar. Characters, Build, Play and Run menus in the middle of the top bar take you to every D&D Toolbox page. The planner also explains the difference between Save (this planner only) and Save as character (your characters across the toolbox).
  • Build from an imported character. When a character imported on the D&D Toolbox Characters page (from D&D Beyond, Foundry VTT or Fight Club 5) is newer than what the planner last saw, it offers to build from it. It asks before replacing a build you already have, and the level order is a guess you can change. The character sheet summary now also gives the numbers at the character's current level when your plan runs ahead of it, plus skills when the import knows them.
  • Three more tools in the footer. The footer now links to Feats & Origins, Rules Reference and Monster Lookup.
  • Feats at every ASI. Each "Ability Score Improvement" and Epic Boon row in the level table links to the new Feats & Origins tool. With a character linked, the link lists only the feats that character qualifies for at that level. Feats you marked as taken there show next to your ASIs in the summary.

November 18, 2026

  • Character sheet summary. A character linked to a build now carries a short summary of it at its full planned level (level, proficiency bonus, hit points and hit dice, saving throws, spell and pact slots, spell save DCs, attacks, resources, ASI levels, feature names and proficiencies) for the D&D Toolbox Character Sheet and Party pages. It updates as you edit and travels with share links.
  • Charts and a little motion. A new "build by level" card charts your spell and pact slots and your average hit points at every character level. Hover, tap or use the arrow keys to read a level, or show the numbers as a table. Cards ease in as you scroll and catch a soft light under the pointer, and the level count, notices and "copied" messages animate gently. With reduced motion turned on, everything simply appears.

November 17, 2026

  • Fighter, Rogue and Monk: all 13 classes. The last three classes for 2014 and 2024 rules, with Action Surge, Indomitable, Second Wind, Sneak Attack, ki or focus points and the martial arts die. Fighters get Extra Attack at 5, 11 and 20. Eldritch Knight and Arcane Trickster count as third casters once you pick them. D&D Beyond imports now bring in every class; only homebrew classes stay as locked levels.
  • Bard, Cleric, Wizard, Ranger and Artificer. Five more classes for 2014 and 2024 rules, with their features, subclasses, spells, spell save DCs (Wisdom, Intelligence or Charisma) and resources such as Bardic Inspiration, Channel Divinity and Arcane Recovery. Rangers count as half casters (from ranger 2 under 2014 rules); the Artificer rounds up, so it casts from level 1. Subclasses that grant Extra Attack (College of Valor, Bladesinging, Battle Smith, Armorer) are counted too.
  • Druid and Barbarian. Both are now full classes for 2014 and 2024 rules: features at every level, subclasses (including Circle of the Moon, Path of the Totem Warrior and 2024's Path of the Wild Heart), Rage count and damage, Wild Shape uses and the highest beast CR. Druids cast with Wisdom, so the spell save DC is now worked out per class. New Moon Barbarian presets for both editions.
  • Up to three classes. Class levels show only the classes in your build, with an "Add a class" menu. A build can mix up to three classes, and the planner says so when a choice would add a fourth. More classes are coming; until then, levels in other classes stay as locked levels.
  • Resistance doesn't stack. A new rules note explains why Rage, a bear totem and a resistant beast form still halve damage only once, what does stack (Uncanny Dodge), and how Rage works with Wild Shape in each edition.

November 15, 2026

  • Share a character with its build. A character's share link (the Share button in the header, or "Share character" here) now carries the whole Multiclass Planner build: level order, subclasses, Pact Boon, locked levels and the level you were viewing. Whoever opens it and adds the character gets the same plan. If they already have a different build for that character, or unsaved work, they choose whether to load the shared build or keep theirs.

November 14, 2026

  • Characters from other sites stay whole. Classes the planner doesn't model, such as Fighter 5 on a Fighter 5 / Paladin 15, now show as locked levels. They count toward your level, proficiency bonus, hit points, Extra Attack, spell slots and the level-20 cap. A subclass the planner doesn't list, like Divine Soul under 2024 rules, is kept rather than erased.
  • Your work is safe. Picking another character no longer replaces an unsaved build: you choose when to load it. If the character changes on another site while you're editing here, you choose which version to keep. Loading an old saved build no longer overwrites its character. Changes are saved to the character after a short pause instead of on every keystroke.
  • Fixes. The 2024 Sorcadin description now matches the 2024 rules. D&D Beyond imports keep classes the planner doesn't cover, so your level and saving throws are right, and short ddb.ac links get a clear message. Build names like "constructor" save properly. Opening a link to a character this browser doesn't have says so.
  • Easier to use. Grey text has more contrast, the 2014/2024 switch works with the arrow keys, closing the import dialog returns you to its button, and screen readers no longer repeat the character panel on every change.

November 13, 2026

  • Characters across the toolbox. Save a build as a character and it appears in the Character menu at the top of every D&D Toolbox site. Edits here keep it up to date, picking a character loads its build, and buttons take it straight to the Spell Planner and Item Finder.
  • Spell Planner in the footer. The footer now links to the new Spell Planner in the D&D Toolbox.
  • Print a build sheet, and tidier links. Printing now gives a clean sheet with your class levels, summary, milestones and the level table, without the buttons and menus. Links shared in chat show a proper preview, a mistyped address shows a helpful "page not found" page, and the header and footer links are easier to tap.
  • Steadier toolbar. Loading a saved build no longer moves the buttons around: the Preset and Saved builds menus keep a fixed width, and the buttons always sit on their own row.
  • Update notice. If the site is updated while you have it open, a small bar offers to reload so you get the new version.
  • Levels per class. The top of the page now has a counter for each class (0 to 20, up to 20 in total), so you set Paladin 6 / Sorcerer 14 directly. Builds can stop below level 20. The level-order grid is still there underneath for choosing which class you take at each level.
  • Import from D&D Beyond. Paste your character link, open its data page and paste it back to load your Paladin, Warlock and Sorcerer levels, subclasses, ability scores and name. It all happens in your browser. D&D Beyond doesn't record the order you took levels, so check the Levels grid afterwards.
  • Presets match the rules. The Preset menu lists only builds for the rules you have selected, 2014 or 2024.
  • Consistent design. The change log moved to its own page, and the header, footer and theme are now shared across the D&D Toolbox sites.
  • Back to the toolbox. A link at the top of the page goes back to the D&D Toolbox.
  • Launch. Paladin, Warlock and Sorcerer for 2014 and 2024 rules: a class per level, ability scores with prerequisite warnings, subclasses at the right level, multiclass spell slots with each edition's rounding, separate Pact Magic, spells, invocations, metamagic, feats, hit points, milestones, saved builds, share links and seven presets.
