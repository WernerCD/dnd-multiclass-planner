Changes

What's new on the Multiclass Planner, newest first.

November 13, 2026

  • Characters across the toolbox. Save a build as a character and it appears in the Character menu at the top of every D&D Toolbox site. Edits here keep it up to date, picking a character loads its build, and buttons take it straight to the Spell Planner and Item Finder.
  • Spell Planner in the footer. The footer now links to the new Spell Planner in the D&D Toolbox.
  • Print a build sheet, and tidier links. Printing now gives a clean sheet with your class levels, summary, milestones and the level table, without the buttons and menus. Links shared in chat show a proper preview, a mistyped address shows a helpful "page not found" page, and the header and footer links are easier to tap.
  • Steadier toolbar. Loading a saved build no longer moves the buttons around: the Preset and Saved builds menus keep a fixed width, and the buttons always sit on their own row.
  • Update notice. If the site is updated while you have it open, a small bar offers to reload so you get the new version.
  • Levels per class. The top of the page now has a counter for each class (0 to 20, up to 20 in total), so you set Paladin 6 / Sorcerer 14 directly. Builds can stop below level 20. The level-order grid is still there underneath for choosing which class you take at each level.
  • Import from D&D Beyond. Paste your character link, open its data page and paste it back to load your Paladin, Warlock and Sorcerer levels, subclasses, ability scores and name. It all happens in your browser. D&D Beyond doesn't record the order you took levels, so check the Levels grid afterwards.
  • Presets match the rules. The Preset menu lists only builds for the rules you have selected, 2014 or 2024.
  • Consistent design. The change log moved to its own page, and the header, footer and theme are now shared across the D&D Toolbox sites.
  • Back to the toolbox. A link at the top of the page goes back to the D&D Toolbox.
  • Launch. Paladin, Warlock and Sorcerer for 2014 and 2024 rules: a class per level, ability scores with prerequisite warnings, subclasses at the right level, multiclass spell slots with each edition's rounding, separate Pact Magic, spells, invocations, metamagic, feats, hit points, milestones, saved builds, share links and seven presets.
